About Dilsha Water

Trusted mineral water, right here in Danta.

Dilsha Water Danta is a local mineral water wholesaler serving Danta and the villages around it in Banaskantha district. We supply purified, quality-checked water in 20L jars and retail bottles — straight to homes, shops, hotels, and events.

Whether you need a single jar refilled at home or a standing wholesale order for your shop, hotel, or function, we keep the supply steady and the rates fair. In a region where water matters more than most places, that reliability is the whole business.
